Connect Four

SETUP:
- Two players use a 7 columns by 6 rows grid.
- One player is X and the other is O.

TURN:
- Players alternate turns.
- On your turn, drop one disc into a column.
- The disc falls to the lowest empty row in that column.

END:
- Win by making 4 in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
- If the board is full with no 4 in a row, the game is a draw.
