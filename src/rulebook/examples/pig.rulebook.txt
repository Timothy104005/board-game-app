Pig Dice

SETUP:
- Two players.
- Target score is 20 points.

TURN:
- On your turn, roll a die.
- If you roll a 1, your turn total is lost and your turn ends.
- Otherwise add the roll to your turn total and choose to roll again or hold.
- Hold banks your turn total into your score and ends your turn.

END:
- First player to reach the target score wins.
