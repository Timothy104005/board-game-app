Mini Splendor

SETUP:
- Two players.
- Start with tokens in a shared supply and a fixed visible market of cards.

TURN:
- On your turn, take 3 different tokens, or take 2 same tokens only when supply for that color is at least 4.
- You may buy card from the market if you can pay cost with your tokens.
- Discounts reduce future card costs.
- Keep your token limit at 10.

SCORING:
- Cards grant points.
- Higher points wins.

NOTES:
- No gold tokens for payment.
- No reserve cards.
- No nobles.
