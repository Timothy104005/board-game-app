Tic Tac Toe

SETUP:
- Use a 3x3 grid.
- Player 1 is X and Player 2 is O.

TURN:
- Players alternate turns.
- On your turn, place your mark in one empty cell.

END:
- Win by making three in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
- If all cells are filled with no three in a row, the game is a draw.
